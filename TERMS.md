# Contributor Terms

> These are a sensible default, not legal advice — have HR/legal sanity-check before running with real candidates.

1. **This is an evaluation.** OpenBuild Gallery is a non-commercial hiring sandbox. Contributing is **optional** and part of an interview process; choosing not to participate will not, by itself, disqualify you — tell us and we'll use a standard take-home instead.
2. **Effort is bounded.** A bounty is scoped to roughly a day. Don't over-invest; we're evaluating how you work, not extracting a product.
3. **Your work, credited.** You retain authorship. Merged contributions are credited to you by name/handle in the gallery and changelog, and you may reference your contribution publicly.
4. **License.** Contributions are made under this repo's license (MIT). Because the project is non-commercial and open-source, your merged code stays public and attributed.
5. **AI is allowed** and must be disclosed in the PR (what you directed vs. decided).
6. **Data & privacy.** Your PRs, review scores, and participation are used only for this hiring decision, kept confidential to the hiring team, and not shared publicly beyond the credit above.
7. **You can withdraw** at any time; we'll close your PRs and remove your data from the evaluation on request.
